The product system

Automation for repetition. People for judgement.

Modules work together around the lead, while technical questions, complaints, exceptions, and special requests are handed to a person.

01

Instant response

A new inquiry receives a relevant response immediately, including outside working hours.

02

Qualification & scoring

Known details are extracted, missing details are asked, and sales readiness is scored.

03

Booking

Qualified leads can move directly to an appropriate appointment or human next step.

04

Follow-up

Open inquiries, quotes, missed calls, and older leads can follow distinct workflows.

05

Pipeline visibility

Source, status, score, owner, and next action remain visible in one process.

06

Review requests

After completed work, the system can request a review at an appropriate moment.

HUMAN HANDOFF

The system knows when to stop.

EXAMPLE ROUTING RULE“The outdoor unit needs a listed-building approval.” → Do not improvise a technical answer. Capture the context and route it to a specialist for confirmation.

First vertical

Starting with HVAC. Designed to expand.

The first pilot focuses on air-conditioning and heat-pump companies, where speed, location, timing, and technical handoff matter. The underlying product is designed for high-value local services more broadly.

TYPICAL LEAD PATH

From “how much?” to a useful sales conversation.

  • Understand what is already knownDo not ask for the 30 m² room size twice.
  • Qualify what changes the next stepLocation, property type, timing, contact, and intent.
  • Route responsiblyBook, follow up, nurture, or hand off — without pretending every lead is HOT.
