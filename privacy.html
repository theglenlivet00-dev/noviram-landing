2 Principles and definitions

2.1. The Controller processes personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently, for specified purposes, to the extent and for the time necessary to achieve those purposes, accurately and with appropriate security measures (Article 5 GDPR).

2.2. Personal data: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (a data subject). Processing: any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure or deletion. Processor: a person or entity that processes personal data on behalf of the controller.

2.3. The Controller's services are intended for business customers; the Controller does not knowingly collect data relating to persons under 16.

3.7. Noviram AI, the corporate Project Assistant and Lead Engine demo

Using AI is voluntary. The browser sends entered messages, relevant conversation history and structured information extracted from them to Noviram's server-side API. In live AI mode, the server uses the OpenAI Responses API to interpret messages, extract project or service facts and prepare a response. Sending contact details entered separately in the form does not require a new OpenAI call; if a visitor enters such data into the AI conversation itself, it may be processed as part of the message.

Server application rules calculate the qualification score and the next suggested system action. Their purpose is to organise the business need and prepare the next useful question or human discussion. Qualification is not a contractual decision or credit assessment and does not create an entitlement to a service or appointment. Automated evaluation linked to personal data may constitute profiling; its GDPR classification and the possible need for an impact assessment require legal review. The application does not make a final decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

The embedded and floating corporate assistants share one browser session. The Lead Engine HVAC demo has a separate session: example requirements may be processed using AI, but the demo does not submit real HVAC leads, send SMS, transfer data to a CRM or book real calendar appointments. The product page's separate business contact option does not send the HVAC demo data.

OpenAI requests use store: false: the application does not request storage of responses for later retrieval through the API. This does not mean the provider retains no data at all; abuse-prevention logging and other provider processing may occur under OpenAI's terms and the actual settings of the company project. Details: OpenAI API data controls. Final verification of settings and contractual safeguards is a pre-launch task.

In the separately labelled local AI test mode, interpretation takes place without an OpenAI call. The precise lawful basis for AI processing, the roles of individual controllers and processors, and retention rules form part of the pre-launch legal review. Do not provide confidential or sensitive personal information.

3.8. Providing data is voluntary, but without the necessary contact details we cannot respond to an enquiry. 6 Data security

6.1. This release uses server-side key handling, input validation, size and request-rate limits, a designated email recipient and data-minimised application logging. Live operation requires HTTPS, restricted access and appropriate secret management. Before launch, Noviram must verify the actual hosting, mailbox, access protection, backup and organisational measures in proportion to the risks under Article 32 GDPR.

6.2. In the event of a personal data breach, the Controller acts under Articles 33–34 GDPR: it records the breach, notifies the supervisory authority of a breach involving risk without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours, and also informs affected data subjects where the breach involves high risk.

7 Data subject rights

Data subjects may exercise the following rights at any time by contacting info@noviram.com. The Controller responds without undue delay and no later than one month after receipt; where justified, this may be extended by two months with notification. Exercising rights is free of charge, except for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

  • Access (Article 15): you may ask whether your data is being processed and request a copy of the data processed.
  • Rectification (Article 16): you may request correction of inaccurate data and completion of incomplete data.
  • Erasure — the “right to be forgotten” (Article 17): you may request deletion, for example where the purpose of processing has ended, consent has been withdrawn or processing is unlawful. Erasure is not possible where processing is legally required, such as accounting retention, or necessary for legal claims.
  • Restriction (Article 18): you may request restriction of processing, for example while the accuracy of disputed data is being verified.
  • Data portability (Article 20): you may receive data processed by automated means on the basis of consent or contract in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or request its transfer to another controller.
  • Objection (Article 21): you may object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time for reasons relating to your particular situation, and without restriction to processing for direct marketing.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Article 7): you may withdraw consent to consent-based processing at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

9 Browser storage and cookies

9.1. The website code does not set analytics or marketing cookies. sessionStorage and localStorage are browser storage mechanisms, not cookies. Functional storage maintains conversation continuity and remembers the visitor's language choice.

sessionStorage
noviramCorporateAgentSession: short conversation history, structured state and a session identifier for the corporate assistant, shared by its embedded and floating interfaces. noviramLeadEngineHvacSession: state of the separate HVAC demo. Each client keeps at most 14 history entries; resetting deletes the relevant conversation. The browser associates storage with the page session; restoring a session may also restore previous data.
localStorage
noviramLang: the HU/EN language choice in the browser until the visitor overwrites it or removes it by clearing site data. It is not an advertising identifier. An explicit language parameter in the URL takes priority; browser-language detection is not used.

9.2. Visitors may clear storage in their browser's site-data settings. Browser deletion does not delete an email already explicitly sent or provider records; requests concerning those records may be submitted under Section 7. The application does not build its own persistent conversation database; temporary server-side processing, provider processing and submitted email are separate data flows.

9.3. There is no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar or other added analytics or advertising tracking. Before introducing such non-essential technology in future, the policy and consent arrangements must be reviewed, and any necessary consent management must be implemented before activation. This release does not add a generic cookie banner solely because AI is present.

10 The Service Provider as processor

10.1. Where the Controller operates AI systems for its clients, such as chatbots, abandoned-cart automation or customer follow-up, that process the personal data of the client's own customers or users, the controller for that processing is the client, and Noviram Digital Kft. acts as processor under Article 28 GDPR. In such cases the Parties enter into a written data processing agreement (DPA). Noviram Digital Kft. processes data only on the client's documented instructions, engages sub-processors only with the client's prior authorisation, and returns or deletes the data, at the client's choice, when the engagement ends.
